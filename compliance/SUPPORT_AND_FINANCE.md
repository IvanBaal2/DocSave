# Support procedure and financial transparency

## Support
- Channel: email to u2648454771@id.gle. In-app: Settings → Contact privacy support (opens the mail app; no data attached).
- Never ask users to send documents, PINs or screenshots of documents.
- Privacy-rights requests: most rights are exercised in-app (Settings → Your privacy rights). For data in the mailbox, answer within one month (GDPR art. 12(3)).
- Response-time commitment and email retention: **to be provided by the publisher**.
- Security reports follow VULNERABILITY_DISCLOSURE.md.

## Financial transparency
- Docsave is **free**. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, advertising, sponsorships, affiliate links or data sales.
- No payment data is processed.
- If monetisation is ever added, the Terms, Privacy Policy, store listings and CRA assessment must be updated first.
