Your private document vault

Docsave stores your passport, ID cards, tickets and important documents encrypted on your own phone. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption on the device, before anything is saved
  • PIN, Face ID / fingerprint, or both (two-factor)
  • Nothing is uploaded, backed up or shared — there are no Docsave servers
  • No advertising, analytics or trackers
  • No recovery: only you can open your vault
